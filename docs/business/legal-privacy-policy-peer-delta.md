## 4a. Optional peer benchmarking (only if you turn it on)

Reverto offers an **optional** feature called **peer benchmarking**. It is **off by default**, and you
get the full core of Reverto — your own true unit costs and comparisons against public USDA market
prices — whether or not you ever turn it on.

**What it does.** If you choose to turn peer benchmarking on, Reverto includes the commodity-level
prices you paid (for example, "chicken breast, dollars per pound," taken from the invoices you already
upload) in an **anonymized, aggregated** benchmark that shows what restaurants like you actually pay.
In return, you can see that benchmark — a blended "what restaurants like you pay" figure — alongside
the USDA market price.

**What we use, and what we do not.** We use only the **parsed, commodity-level unit price** from your
invoices. We do **not** include your business name, your location beyond your general region, your
distributor or account numbers, your invoice numbers, or any individual price line that could be
traced back to you.

**How it is kept anonymous.** We only ever display a **median** (a middle value) — never any single
restaurant's price. We only show a peer figure when **at least 5 different restaurants** (and enough
separate line items) are in the sample, so no single business's prices can be identified or
reverse-engineered from the number. Peer figures are shown by **region only**, reflect **past
prices**, and are never shared with suppliers or shown as a named competitor's price.

**It is opt-in and revocable.** Peer benchmarking is entirely optional. You turn it on with a
dedicated setting (separate from signing up or accepting our Terms), and you can turn it off at any
time in your settings or by emailing us at **revertoo.ino@gmail.com**. When you turn it off, we stop
including your prices in any new benchmarks.

**Peer benchmarking and your right to delete.** If you turn the feature off, or ask us to delete your
data, we stop using your prices going forward and delete or de-identify your underlying data through
our normal deletion process. Prices you previously contributed are combined into anonymized medians
made up of at least 5 restaurants; because those medians are aggregated and cannot be traced back to
any individual business, they are treated as de-identified, and we may not recalculate benchmarks that
were already produced. You are removed from all future benchmarks, and each benchmark stops reflecting
your data the next time it is recalculated.

**Not a sale or share.** We do not sell your prices and we do not disclose them to any third party for
that party's own use. Peer benchmarks stay within Reverto and are only ever surfaced as anonymized
aggregates.
